About Me

I am a Physicist and Data Engineer bridging the gap between fundamental research and modern software engineering. Currently, I am pursuing my M.Sc. in Physics Engineering at Istanbul Technical University (ITU) while serving as an active member of the CMS experiment at CERN, specifically contributing to the HGCAL (High Granularity Calorimeter) project.

In parallel to my academic research, I work as a Data Engineer and Data Scientist. This dual role allows me to apply industrial grade data processing techniques to complex physics problems. My focus is on optimizing analysis workflows to handle petabyte scale datasets efficiently and integrating deep learning models into standard physics analysis pipelines.

Driven by a deep passion for mathematics and physics, I enjoy tackling challenging problems that require a rigorous analytical approach and innovative computational solutions.
