Research

Within CMS, my work centers on the High Granularity Calorimeter (HGCAL). The main themes below will be expanded as results and write-ups become available.

CMS LS3 upgrade & installation

I contribute to the team responsible for HGCAL installation as part of the CMS LS3 upgrade.

Common-mode noise & test-beam analysis

In a test-beam analysis group, I focus on common-mode noise in HGCAL silicon layers and the HGCROC readout, with the goal of reducing and correcting it.

Noise correction methods

I study highly correlated (coherent) noise patterns and compare classical approaches such as ordinary least squares (OLS) with machine-learning methods. The analysis draws on hardware layout, synthetic data simulations, and theoretical noise limits. This work is being shaped as a central topic of my master’s thesis.
